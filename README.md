# Bad Mobs — Stop Annoying Mobs From Spawning

Bad Mobs 9minecraft Mod gives you complete control over which creatures can spawn in your world. It stops specific monsters or animals from appearing naturally, coming out of spawners, or being created by players. The mod works instantly and applies to every creature in the game, including those added by other content packs.

Total Spawn Control: You decide exactly which monsters and animals are allowed to exist in your world.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Darkhax's Minecraft Mods**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
